# KVKK Information Notice Draft

The purpose of this processing activity is to provide privacy-preserving
video analytics and to reduce identifiable visual information before
downstream analysis. The system detects faces in an authorized camera stream
and applies an anonymization transformation in memory. Original frames are
not stored by the application. Processing metadata may be recorded for
security, reliability, and audit purposes without storing image pixels.

The data subject may exercise the rights provided by the Turkish Personal Data
Protection Law No. 6698 through the data controller's designated application
channel. The controller, legal basis, retention period, recipient groups, and
contact address must be completed by the responsible organization before this
draft is used as an official notice.
